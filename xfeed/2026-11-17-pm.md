# Twitter analysis — Tuesday, November 17, 2026, evening
*Covers about 13:05 to 18:10 ET (read about 18:15–18:50 ET). 18 of 23 accounts posted. @zerohedge hit the 30-post cap, so it was read back only to about 16:45 ET and its earlier afternoon posts are missing. No login wall or rate limit. Context: the 16:16 ET closing brief, the November 17 macro outlook and the midday digest.*

**Bottom line:** Nvidia's numbers turned the mood after the close. It earned $2.68 a share on $115.8B of revenue against about $2.48 and $109B expected, guided Q4 to about $130B, raised the FY2028 growth outlook to 75% from 70% and added $150B to its buyback. The stock rose 3–5% after hours, and @zerohedge and @KobeissiLetter say Nasdaq 100 futures and QQQ were up as much as 1.3%. The overhang is not gone: the board says it won't bring back Jensen Huang, the SEC has opened a civil probe, Muddy Waters disclosed a short, and the call showed $390B of supply commitments and $118B of guarantees. Before the report, the session was the bottom-watchers' day. The S&P tested its 200-day (@TheProfInvestor, @Bluekurtic), VIX hit about 27 and @GAndersonTrades refused to open new shorts with VIX above 25. **Watch:** whether QQQ holds above the 661.5 VolumeLeaders level at the open, then the FOMC minutes at 14:00 ET.
