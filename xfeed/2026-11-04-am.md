Nasdaq futures fell about 2% after the polls closed (@DeItaone, @KobeissiLetter) and QQQ traded at $668–671 (@Mr_Derivatives), then everything reversed. At the open the Dow was up over 1,100 points, S&P +1.7%, Nasdaq 100 +2.0%, silver +5% and gold +3%, while WTI fell to about $93 (@KobeissiLetter, @DeItaone). Most accounts call it "sell the rumor, buy the news" (@KobeissiLetter, @Bluekurtic, @kpak82). Leadership is not semis, though. Amazon is +7% and Microsoft +5.7%, but SOXX is -30% from its high (@Mr_Derivatives). Tesla and SpaceX are down 4–5% on Musk's political exposure, and Goldman says data-center moratoria could hit 45% of planned capacity (@DeItaone). The level to watch is QQQ 700 (@Mr_Derivatives: "almost $700 again"), which matches our 699.43 trigger.

## Themes

Losers: TSLA (-4–5.6%) and SpaceX (SPCX at $129 lows; @Mr_Derivatives), plus data centers, where Goldman flags moratorium risk (@DeItaone). @zerohedge sums it up as "Big Tech hugs the Sierra Club" and adds DDOG -17% on a beat and raise (@TrendSpider) and AMD giving back its AMD-earnings pop (-2% pre-market).
- **Oil and metals.** WTI fell to $93 (-12% in 4 days; @KobeissiLetter). A US drone was reported shot down over Qeshm (IRGC via @DeItaone), Pakistan sees progress with Iran, and the UAE is weighing a third pipeline that bypasses Hormuz. Gold topped $4,000 and silver reached $52.26 (@zerohedge, @DeItaone).
- **Data is firm, not hot.** ADP came in at +86K vs 67K expected (@zerohedge, @DeItaone). The trade deficit narrowed to $105.0B. Final services PMI was 56.1 vs a 57.9 flash. Treasury held refunding at $125B but dropped "at least" from its coupon guidance, opening the door to larger auctions in 2027 (@zerohedge).
- **Contrarian voices.** "Expect a 5% market return" under a Republican president with a Democratic Congress (@asklivermore). @kpak82 says SPY rallied right into its 20/50 SMA and that semis, the sector retail bought the dip on, opened red. @TheProfInvestor called the overnight drop "a reset that was needed" and says "this week we bottom."
