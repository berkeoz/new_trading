# Twitter analysis — Monday, October 12, 2026, evening
**Bottom line:** The accounts spent the session watching chips break while the index barely moved. In @kpak82's words, semis got "wacked" while SPY was flat. Three explanations were offered: Microsoft's Maia 300 chip becoming its main AI system (@unusual_whales), Broadcom CEO Hock Tan calling many AI investors gamblers (@Mr_Derivatives, @unusual_whales), and Cleveland Fed President Hammack saying policy is "not restrictive enough" (@DeItaone). Gulf risk stayed loud: booms in Riyadh, a third tanker hit in Hormuz, and another vessel struck in the afternoon (@DeItaone, @zerohedge). The mood was defensive, with failed breakouts everywhere on the technicians' charts. For tomorrow, watch whether NVDA holds its breakout retest (@TheProfInvestor) and whether MU holds its 50-day (@Mr_Derivatives). Both feed straight into our SOXX 543.34 trendline before Wednesday's CPI.
