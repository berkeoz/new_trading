**Bottom line:** One story drove the day: Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after pressure from President Trump. NVDA rose about 31–32%, which @KobeissiLetter calls the largest one-day market-cap gain in history (+$955B), and it pulled the S&P up 4.7% to its best day since April 2025. Our brief has SPY +4.74% at 722.51, QQQ +7.0% and SOXX +13.6%. The mood went from capitulation to euphoria, but most of the careful accounts warn against chasing. @Bluekurtic's base rates for gap-ups this size are poor, @Mr_Derivatives and @GAndersonTrades say SPY and QQQ are still under their 200-day averages, and @kpak82 says this is "probably not" a major bottom. **For tomorrow:** watch whether SPY holds 713.5 and can close above 727.97 / 730.47, and whether QQQ can close above 657.50. Dell reports Tuesday. New risks: the Megaspeed export blacklist, a Senate Huang hearing (Dec 3 per DeItaone; our calendar has Dec 2), and AMD registering 336.9M shares for possible sale.
