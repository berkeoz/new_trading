The Cleveland Fed survey shows CEOs' inflation expectations rising. Against that, crude fell about $2 on Hormuz flows, Goldman warns of a 2027 oil glut, and gas averages $3.84.
- **13F season** (@unusual_whales, @zerohedge). Druckenmiller exited AMD and ARM, dumped GOOGL and SNDK, and bought STX, NEM, APP and PLTR. Berkshire bought BSP and added LEN, DAL and M. Lone Pine cut AMAT 92%. Appaloosa added NVDA and bought ORCL. Pershing bought NFLX.
