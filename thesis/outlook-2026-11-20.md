# 6-12 month outlook — November 20, 2026

## The regime ahead
The economy is still running hot. Q3 GDP grew 3.4% annualized. Payrolls rose 165k in October, unemployment is 4.1% and jobless claims are near 200k. Inflation is not cooling: core PCE is 3.1%, core CPI is 3.8% annualized over 3 months, and PPI is 6.5% y/y.

The Fed is hiking. Futures fully price a 25bp move on December 9 and about 4.5% by mid-2027. Governor Cook said waiting longer "risks getting behind the curve."

What changed is the market regime. The AI trade is being de-rated hard and fast. A negative equity risk premium and a stock-market-to-GDP ratio at the 98th percentile leave little cushion. Our base case for the next 6-12 months is a choppy, range-bound market. It would have higher real yields, lower valuations for long-duration growth, and leadership from cash-generating defensives, energy and financials that earn more from higher rates and volatility.

## Risks to the view
- **A sharp rebound:** extreme fear (VIX backwardation, Fear & Greed 25, AAII bears 42%) can spark a violent rebound in AI and growth stocks. The defensive tilt would lag it.
- **Credit:** a credit-led deleveraging would hit everything, including insurers and exchanges.
- **Inflation:** a hot October PCE (Nov 25) or a 50bp December hike would pressure high-P/E defensives (WMT 39.6x, KO and staples in the high 20s).
- **Oil:** an Iran deal or OPEC+ supply increase would break the energy theme below WTI $80.
- **Health care policy:** drug-pricing or CMS rule changes.
