# 6-12 month outlook — November 2, 2026

## The regime ahead
The base case for the next 6-12 months is still energy-led stagflation-lite. Core PCE is 3.1% y/y, PPI 6.3% y/y and UMich 1-year inflation expectations 4.6%. Payrolls average only +51k over three months, but claims of 194k show no layoffs. Fed funds futures price a December 9 hike at 80%. The 10Y is 4.89%, and the 10Y real yield (~2.6%) is near a 10-year high. With market cap/GDP at 256% (97th percentile) and a negative equity risk premium, multiples have little room to expand. Leadership should keep coming from cash-generative, rate-insensitive businesses: energy, quality health care, defensive retail, insurers and defense. Long-duration AI and semiconductor names stay fragile.

## Risks to the view
- A durable Iran deal takes oil below $85: energy names lose their premium and long-duration growth rebounds.
- The Fed overtightens into a labor contraction (claims above 250k), hurting insurers and retailers.
- The AI de-rating spreads into a broad drawdown that also pulls down defensives (valuations at the 97th percentile).
- A sharp bond rally (10Y below 4.6%) would reward the growth exposure the portfolio avoids.
- Midterm outcomes that raise drug-pricing or energy-policy uncertainty.
