The 6 entry rules (20 pts each):
R1 RSI(14) < 40 — stock is oversold on momentum
R2 MACD histogram < 0 but rising — selling pressure is easing
R3 Stochastic %K crosses above %D while both < 50 — early bullish flip
R4 Volume above 20-day average — buyers stepping in with conviction

Exit Signals (shown when you should consider selling)
↑ PROFIT TAKE
Stock has run up and is now overbought. RSI > 70 and falling, MACD histogram turning negative from above zero, or Stochastic doing a bearish cross from overbought. If you entered on a BUY signal and the stock has gained — this is your sell signal.
⬇ STOP LOSS
Price has broken below its 200-day moving average — the long-term trend has reversed. If you're holding a position, this is a hard exit to protect capital.
⚠ CAUTION
Momentum is weakening (MACD bearish, OBV falling, or price slipping below MA20 on volume) but the stock is not yet overbought. Not a sell signal by itself — watch closely for a PROFIT TAKE to follow.
