# Macro outlook — October 29, 2026
**Big picture:** The regime is unchanged: strong growth, sticky inflation and a labor market that barely hires but does not fire. Today's data confirmed it.
- **Growth:** Q3 GDP grew 3.4% annualized, above the 3.1–3.2% consensus.
- **Inflation:** September core PCE was +0.3% m/m and 3.1% y/y, exactly as expected. Headline PCE was 3.6% y/y.
- **Labor:** claims fell to 194K.

Futures still price a December 9 hike at 86% and about two more hikes by mid-2027. With no inflation surprise, though, the 10Y fell back below 5% (4.97%).

For markets, this means:
- **Equities:** supported by earnings and growth; leadership is rotating toward rate-sensitive and defensive groups.
- **Bonds:** range-bound near 5%.
- **Dollar:** firm.
- **Oil:** about $100, with two-way Iran risk.

## What changed
- **Q3 GDP (advance):** 3.4% vs 3.1–3.2% expected and 2.2% in Q2 ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/29/pce-inflation-accelerates-to-3-6-as-gdp-growth-beats-estimates-jobless-claims-fall/), [SIA Wealth](https://www.siawealth.com/2026/10/29/alphabet-disappointment-weighs-on-us-futures-as-q3-growth-accelerates/)). It matches the Atlanta Fed GDPNow's ~3.5% track.
- **September PCE:**
  - Headline +0.5% m/m (consensus +0.5%) and 3.6% y/y (consensus 3.6%), up from 3.4%.
  - Core +0.3% m/m and 3.1% y/y, both in line, up from 3.0%. The data feed shows core at 3.12% vs 3.03%.
  - Energy explains the gap between headline and core.
- **Income and spending:** income +0.2% vs +0.3% expected; spending +0.5% vs +0.6%. Spending is slightly softer, and real income growth is being squeezed by inflation.
- **Jobless claims:** 194K vs 199K expected; continuing claims 1.701M (-21K).
- **Market reaction:**
  - The front end barely moved (13-week bill -0.8bp) while the belly and long end rallied: 5Y -8.4bp, 10Y -7.3bp, 30Y -5.2bp. Gilts and Iran-talks headlines helped.
  - Stocks split. Tech fell on Alphabet; health care, financials, homebuilders and small caps rose.
- **Versus yesterday's outlook (Oct 28):** the base case is confirmed.
  - The "hot PCE" downside trigger (core at 0.3%+ m/m) technically printed, but it was fully expected, so it did not move pricing.
  - The 10Y's slip below 5% slightly reduces the stagflation-plus-valuation risk for now.

## Pillars
**Inflation:**
- **Data:** headline CPI is 3.72% y/y (75th 10-year percentile; 3-month annualized 4.6%), core CPI 2.57% (50th), PCE 3.65% (78th), core PCE 3.12% (69th; 3-month annualized 2.9%). PPI is 6.35% (82nd; 3-month annualized 8.1%), so pipeline pressure is still building.
- **Expectations:** 5-year breakevens are 2.49% (78th) and UMich 1-year expectations 4.6%.
- **Trend:** headline up from 2.4% in January. That is above 2019's ~1.5–2% and far below the 2022 peak near 7% PCE. It is energy-led: core is steady, headline is not.

**Labor:**
- **Data:** payrolls +29K in September (3-month average +51K, 12th percentile). Unemployment is 4.2% (Sahm 0.00). Claims are 194K, near a 10-year low (2nd percentile), and continuing claims 1.70M.
- **Openings and wages:** JOLTS 7.08M (36th). Wages 3.0% y/y (21st, falling). The latest ADP print is September at +90K ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/private-sector-jobs-rose-by-90000-in-september-better-than-expected-adp-reports.html)).
- **What it means:** wages are not driving inflation.

**Growth:**
- **Data:**
  - GDP 3.4%, retail sales +6.3% y/y, industrial production +2.2%, durable goods +9.9% y/y. Empire (25.5) and Philly (20.9) are expanding.
  - The latest ISM readings (September) are 54.5 for manufacturing and 54.9 for services ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/ism-non-manufacturing-pmi-54-9-versus-55-2-estimate/)).
  - Housing is weak: starts 1.22M (15th percentile), existing sales 3.99M (8th).
  - Sentiment is split: UMich 48.1 (1st percentile) vs Conference Board 91.7 in October ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-consumer-confidence-increased-in-october-302920731.html)).
  - The Conference Board leading index is n/a.

**Fed & rates:**
- **Policy rate:** fed funds is 4.00% (upper) and core PCE 3.1%, a real rate of about +0.9pp. That is mildly restrictive.
- **Yields:** the 2Y is 4.70% (90th). The 10Y is 4.97% live (98th percentile on the Oct 27 reading of 4.99%). The 10Y TIPS real yield is 2.64% (98th).
- **Curve:** 10Y−2Y +0.38pp and 10Y−3M +0.87pp, both normal.

**Financial conditions:**
- **Data:** the Chicago Fed index is -0.49, loose. The HY spread is 3.18pp (62nd), up from 2.63 in August. M2 is +5.7% y/y. The Fed's balance sheet is $6.75T.
- **What it means:** conditions are loose enough that the Fed sees room to tighten.

**Recession signals:**
- **Data:** Sahm 0.00, NY Fed 12-month odds 13.3%, CFNAI 3-month average +0.03, Chauvet-Piger 0.62%.
- **What it means:** none is flashing; Q3 growth argues against recession in the near term.

**Valuation:**
- **Buffett indicator:** 261% of GDP, a 10-year high (97th percentile). The dot-com peak was 163% on the same measure, so it is far above that.
- **Risk premium:** equity risk premiums vs the 10Y are negative: SPY -0.88pp, QQQ -1.33pp, SOXX -1.66pp.
- **What it means:** misses get punished hard, as Alphabet's ~10% drop shows.

**Oil:**
- **Prices:** WTI spot is $103.4 (+67% y/y, 96th percentile) and futures $100.1 today. Brent spot is $123 (EIA). OVX is 58.6 (92nd).
- **Inventories:** the latest EIA report showed a 1.9M-barrel crude draw, with stocks 4% below the 5-year average ([Oilprice](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Crude-Oil/US-Oil-Inventories-Fall-Again-As-Prices-Rise.html)).
- **Drivers today:** Iran diplomacy (Vance outreach) and a European stock release push down; IRGC reports of tankers hitting mines in Hormuz push up ([ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/tech-futures-tumble-alphabet-plunges-small-caps-rise-ahead-pce-ecb)).

- **Other pricing:** pre-data reports put the market at ~80% for December and ~70bp of tightening by next September ([ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/tech-futures-tumble-alphabet-plunges-small-caps-rise-ahead-pce-ecb)). Goldman expects hikes to resume in December ([Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/daily-us-equity-opening-news---goog-slides-on-weaker-search-ads-msft-up-after-results-ssnlf-falls-on-weak-profit-lack-of-shareholder-return-details-now-plunges-on-weak-subscriptions-mrvl-nears-acquisition-of-lite-ebay-up-after-earningsgmv-beat)). Today's in-line PCE leaves the Nov 6 jobs report and November CPI as the swing inputs.

## What it means for markets
- **Equities:** strong nominal growth supports earnings, but with negative risk premiums any miss is costly.
- **Rate-sensitive groups:** homebuilders, REITs, regional banks and small caps rally whenever the 10Y dips below 5%. They are trading vehicles, not trends, while hikes are priced.
- **Tech and semis:** hostage to AI capex and free cash flow (Alphabet negative, Microsoft strong) and to the 10Y.
- **Energy:** keeps support from $100 oil.
- **Health care and staples:** lead on a defensive bid (health care +10pp vs SPY over 3 months).
- **Curve, dollar and oil:** a modestly steep curve; a dollar firm on US growth outperformance; oil $95–115.

## Outlook and scenarios (next 3–6 months)
- **Base case (~55%), sticky inflation with one or two more hikes:**
  - December hike to 4.00–4.25%, then a pause. The 10Y is 4.8–5.3%; equities chop with rotating leadership.
  - *Confirmed by:* core PCE at 0.2–0.3% m/m, payrolls 0–100K, HY below 3.5pp, WTI $90–110.
- **Upside case (~15%), soft landing:**
  - An Iran deal reopens Hormuz, WTI falls below $85, core cools to 0.2% or lower, the December hike is priced out and the 10Y drops below 4.7%; breadth broadens.
  - *Confirmed by:* a signed Iran deal, falling breakevens, and IWM and transports leading.
- **Downside case (~30%), stagflation plus a valuation or credit scare:**
  - Post-midterm strikes push Brent above $125; hikes run into 2027; AI-capex funding strain spreads.
  - *Confirmed by:* core at 0.4%+, HY above 3.75pp, a 10Y above 5.3%, claims above 250K.
