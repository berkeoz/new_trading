# Macro outlook — November 12, 2026
**Big picture:** The regime is "overheat": strong growth, a firm labor market and sticky inflation. October CPI cemented a December 9 hike.
- **CPI:** core rose 0.3% for the third straight month, exactly Waller's bar for weighing 50bp. Futures now price the Dec 9 meeting at 4.17% (+29bp): a certain 25bp plus a small chance of 50bp, with about 4.7% by mid-2027.
- **Stocks:** a 5.07% 10Y and a negative equity risk premium leave little room. Leadership has rotated from AI into financials, cyclicals and defensives.
- **Dollar:** at a 3-month high.
- **Oil:** Brent fell to the low $90s on Iran diplomacy, then bounced when Trump said a deal is not needed.

- **October CPI (released Nov 10):** ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/economic-data/26/11/63088120/us-cpi-october-consumer-prices-inflation-fed-rate-hike), [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/october-cpi-runs-hotter-than-expected-as-core-inflation-hits-wallers-threshold-for-a-half-point-hike/))
  - Headline +0.4% m/m vs +0.3% expected (prior +0.6%). Core +0.3% vs +0.2% (prior +0.3%).
  - Shelter rose 0.3% (after 0.4%) and made up over 30% of the monthly gain. Food also pushed it up ([US Real Estate Investor](https://www.unitedstatesrealestateinvestor.com/bls-cpi-october-2026-prices-rose-0-4-percent-shelter/)).
  - **No official 12-month rate:** BLS did not publish one because it never collected October 2025 data ([24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/10/feds-waller-set-a-half-point-hike-threshold-and-core-inflation-just-hit-it-heres-what-it-does-to-a-retired-couples-450000-in-cds-and-money-market-funds/)). Our feed carries headline 3.72% y/y and core 2.57%; core's 3-month annualized pace is 3.8%.
- **Market reaction:**
  - The 10Y rose about 8bp on Nov 10 and crossed 5%. After the Veterans Day bond holiday it is 5.07% today, with the 5Y at 4.92% (+9.3bp).
  - Logan called for "at least two more" hikes. FedWatch moved to about 88–90% for 25bp and 10–12% for 50bp (sources conflict) ([MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/why-the-fed-is-weighing-a-december-rate-hike-the-data-the-votes-and-what-a-half-point-move-would-mean/)).
  - DXY is at 103.08, a 3-month high, and gold is at a 3-month low.
- **Since then:**
  - Initial claims were 200K (prior 191K) and existing home sales 3.75M (the low of the 10-year data).
  - Nvidia's CEO resigned on Nov 12, an equity-specific shock rather than a macro one.
- **Versus the Nov 6 outlook:** the base case (a December hike) is now certain in pricing. The debate has moved to its **size** and to **how many in 2027**: futures add about 50bp more by July. The downside case (core 0.4%+) did not trigger, but the upside case (oil-led disinflation) got weaker after Trump's Iran post.

## Pillars
**Inflation:**
- **Data:** CPI 3.72% y/y (75th 10-year percentile) and core CPI 2.57% (50th), but core's 3-month annualized pace is 3.8%. PCE is 3.65% and core PCE 3.12% (69th). PPI is 6.35% y/y (82nd; 3-month annualized 8.1%), which keeps pipeline pressure high.
- **Expectations:** 5-year breakevens eased to 2.25%, and UMich 1-year expectations were 4.6% in September (4.3% in the preliminary November reading).
- **Context:** inflation is well above 2019's ~2% but far below the 2022 peak near 9%. Direction: sticky, with services and shelter steady and energy now the swing factor.

**Labor:**
- **Data:** payrolls +165K in October (3-month average +137K), unemployment 4.1% (47th percentile; Sahm 0.00), claims 200K (5th percentile), continuing claims 1.70M (22nd and falling), JOLTS 7.52M.
- **Wages:** 3.1% y/y (23rd), but the 3-month annualized pace is 3.9%.
- **Other surveys:** ADP +86K and ISM services 54.5 (prices 73.0) in early November. The Conference Board index: n/a this cycle.
- **What it means:** firm, giving the Fed no reason to pause.

**Growth:**
- **Data:** Q3 GDP 3.4% annualized, retail sales +6.3% y/y (79th), industrial production +2.2%, durable goods +10% y/y.
- **Surveys:** ISM manufacturing 55.5, Empire 25.5 (92nd), Philly 20.9.
- **Housing:** the weak spot. Starts 1.22M (15th) and existing sales 3.75M (0th percentile), with mortgage rates tied to a 5% 10Y.
- **Consumer sentiment:** near record lows (48.1 in September; 51.0 preliminary November).

**Fed & rates:**
- **Policy rate:** fed funds 4.00% against core PCE 3.1%, a real rate of about +0.9pp, mildly restrictive.
- **Yields:** 2Y 4.56% (88th), 10Y about 5.07% (97th), 10Y TIPS 2.66% (98th).
- **Curve:** 10Y−2Y +0.25pp, flattening from 0.71 in December. The hiking cycle is compressing the front end.

**Financial conditions:**
- **Data:** the Chicago Fed index is -0.46 (loose) and M2 is +5.7% y/y. The HY spread is 3.30pp (78th), off its 3.44 October high; the HY ETF is 77.11 vs its 76.41 3-month low.
- **What it means:** credit is calm. Equity volatility is moderate (VIX 18).

**Recession signals:**
- **Data:** Sahm 0.00, NY Fed 12-month odds 16.7% (up from 13.4%), CFNAI 3-month average +0.03, Chauvet-Piger 0.4%.
- **What it means:** none is flashing.

**Valuation:**
- **Buffett indicator:** 258% of GDP, the 97th percentile and a new high in the data, versus the 163% dot-com peak on the same measure.
- **Risk premium:** negative. SPY's earnings yield is 0.92pp below the 10Y and QQQ's is 1.52pp below. That leaves no cushion for rate or earnings shocks.

**Oil:**
- **Prices:** WTI futures $86.8 and Brent $93.8. Spot WTI was $99.6 on Nov 3 (+70% y/y, 92nd percentile) and has since fallen. OVX is 51 (81st).
- **Inventories:** the last EIA report (week to Oct 30) showed a draw of about 0.9M barrels vs an expected build. This week's report is holiday-shifted, and its result is n/a at writing.
- **Drivers:** Trump's "we don't need a deal" post put a floor under crude. OPEC+ meets Nov 29.

## Fed path
- **Futures (effective rate 3.88%):** Dec 9 implied 4.17% (+29bp), a 100% hike. The implied average is 4.41% in March 2027, 4.66% in June and 4.70% in July. That is roughly two more 25bp hikes after December.
- **FedWatch (Nov 10–11):** about 88.5–89.8% for 25bp and 10.2–11.5% for 50bp. That matches the futures' +29bp.
- **Speakers:**
  - Waller, Logan, Hammack and Barkin favour hiking. Logan wants at least two more, and Waller floated 50bp.
  - Barr has avoided committing ([Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/primer---todays-fedspeak-includes-barr-williams-hammack-schmid-goolsbee-daly)). Williams calls inflation too high.
  - The Reuters poll expects one more hike in December ([Yahoo/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fed-raise-rates-once-more-124512933.html)).
- **Next:** October PPI (Nov 13), October PCE and November payrolls come before the meeting. November CPI lands the day after it.

## What it means for markets
- **Equities:** index upside is capped near the 50-day averages while the 10Y holds 5%. Breadth is improving: equal-weight, transports and financials are risk-on.
- **Tech and semis:** the most exposed (negative risk premium, AI-capex doubts). Now they also carry Nvidia governance and export-control risk.
- **Financials:** banks and insurers gain from a higher front end; the rotation data says financials are "favoured and turning".
- **Energy:** a hedge against a stalled Iran deal.
- **Small caps and homebuilders:** suffer when the 5Y rises. Small caps fell 1.4% today.
- **Defensives:** health care and staples still lead over 3 months.
- **Curve:** bear-flattening bias.
- **Dollar:** firm.
- **Oil:** headline-driven, in the $85–100 range.

## Outlook and scenarios (next 3–6 months)
- **Base case (~55%), overheat with two or three more hikes:**
  - A 25bp hike Dec 9, then one or two more by mid-2027. The 10Y is 4.8–5.3%; equities range-bound with value, financials and energy leadership.
  - *Confirmed by:* core CPI 0.2–0.3% m/m, payrolls above 100K, claims under 230K, HY under 3.75pp.
- **Upside case (~15%), oil-led disinflation:**
  - An Iran deal after all, WTI below $80, headline CPI under 3%. The Fed stops after December or one more hike, and the 10Y falls below 4.6%.
  - *Confirmed by:* a signed deal, breakevens under 2.1%, core at 0.2% for two months.
- **Downside case (~30%), stagflation or a valuation unwind:**
  - Core 0.4%+ or PPI pass-through brings a 50bp move and a 10Y above 5.3%. The AI de-rating (Nvidia) spreads to the cap-weighted indexes and credit.
  - *Confirmed by:* the HY ETF below 76.41, SPY below its 200-day (728.98), Brent above $110.
