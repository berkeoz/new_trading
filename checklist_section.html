Verdict labels

BuyUptrend, entry near the 21-day average or support, reward/risk of at least 2:1, market not risk-off, no event right ahead.
Buy on dipGood trend, wrong spot: wait for the level named in the card (21-day average / support).
HoldIf you own it, keep it; not a fresh entry (stretched, or little room to resistance).
WaitNo buy yet: trend mixed, or a downtrend that is oversold and may be turning; wait for confirmation.
WarningSetup deteriorating: below the 50-day average, or several selling alerts on. Tighten stops, don't add.
Take profitUptrend but overbought and rolling over, or at the target: trim / sell part.
SellDowntrend (below falling averages, Weinstein stage 4) or thesis broken: exit / avoid.

Discipline

  • Decide the stop and the target before buying; write down why you bought (the thesis) and what would prove it wrong.
  • Risk ≤ 1% of the account per position; add only to winners, never average down a downtrend.
  • Take part of the profit at the target and trail the rest with the 21-day average or the trailing stop.
  • Reduce size into earnings, CPI and FOMC days; cash is a position when the market regime is risk-off.
